FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ThumbnailPro.

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail?
Simply paste the YouTube video URL into the search box and click "Get Thumbnails". All available quality options will appear — click Download to save any of them to your device.
Is ThumbnailPro completely free?
Yes! ThumbnailPro is 100% free to use with no registration required, no download limits, and no watermarks added to your images.
What thumbnail qualities are available?
We fetch four quality levels: Default (120×90), Medium Quality (320×180), High Quality (480×360), and Max Resolution (1280×720). Note that not all videos have max resolution thumbnails available.
Can I use the downloaded thumbnails commercially?
YouTube thumbnails are the intellectual property of the video creator. Please ensure you have the appropriate rights or permissions before using any downloaded thumbnails for commercial purposes.
What YouTube URL formats are supported?
We support all common formats: standard youtube.com/watch?v=ID links, youtu.be/ID short links, and youtube.com/embed/ID embed links.
Why is Max Resolution not showing for some videos?
Not all YouTube videos have a maxresdefault.jpg thumbnail. This depends on when the video was uploaded and whether the uploader's channel has the HD thumbnail option enabled.
